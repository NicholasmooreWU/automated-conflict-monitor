# Legal Information & Disclaimers

## ✅ What This Project Does

This is an **educational/research tool** that:
- Collects publicly available news data via NewsAPI
- Analyzes text using open-source NLP libraries
- Visualizes relationships between entities mentioned in news
- Does NOT create original news content

## 📋 Compliance Checklist

### ✅ NewsAPI Terms of Service
- **Data Source**: All news data obtained through [NewsAPI.org](https://newsapi.org)
- **Attribution**: News content copyright belongs to original publishers
- **Usage**: Review [NewsAPI Terms](https://newsapi.org/terms) for commercial vs. non-commercial use
- **Rate Limits**: Free tier limited to 100 requests/day
- **Recommendation**: Upgrade to paid plan if using for commercial purposes

### ✅ Fair Use & Copyright
- **Headlines & Snippets**: Generally okay under fair use (informational/analytical)
- **Full Articles**: NOT republished - only metadata and summaries stored
- **Transformative Use**: Adds analytical value (entity extraction, sentiment, graphs)
- **Attribution**: Original sources linked and credited
- **No Plagiarism**: Content analyzed, not copied verbatim
